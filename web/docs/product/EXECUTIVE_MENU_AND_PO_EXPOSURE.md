# Executive menu and PO exposure - release 045

Menu & prices renders the profitability dashboard by default, with an explicit catalogue view for master maintenance. Scope picks a usable saved sales channel when none is supplied. Business/outlet targets and missing states are evaluated before filtering and pagination; four summary cards cover the full searched scope. Loss and food-cost exceptions sort first. Search/status remain visible; outlet/channel/date filters expand. Supporting recipe, version controls, price/tax details and price history sit behind disclosures. Detail-to-comparison navigation retains selected scope, search, page and status.

Monetary margin is labelled Est. Contribution Margin and its percentage Gross Profit Margin. Both explicitly use the currently connected ingredient/packaging basis, not full contribution after other variable costs or actual operating profit. Existing estimate calculations and historical evidence are unchanged.

Migration 045 snapshots tax-exclusive, after-discount unit costs at PO issue, against the latest eligible purchase invoice (or labelled initial reference) known at that time. Existing issued orders are backfilled with the same temporal cutoffs, without modifying orders, prices or stock. Missing baselines remain missing. Snapshots are private and insert-only through the checked issue function; an issue retry cannot overwrite them.

The executive briefing adds issued-PO warnings, selected by issue date in the business timezone. Active GRNs show actual accepted quantities. Current, revision-matching invoice links show billed base quantities. Unbilled potential = max(ordered - valid billed, 0) times the positive net quote increase. Fully billed rows have zero potential even if delivery is pending. Stale links remain flagged; approved invoice/GRN voids change live progress. Invoice period inflation remains a separate comparison and the two values are not totalled. Only recorded invoices/approved corrections change monthly recipe-cost estimates.

Potentially affected menu recipes trace pinned sub-recipes and linked processed SKUs, with cycle/depth safeguards. This is a dependency warning, not a forecast of menu-level margin change. Scope is the PO outlet/brand and active menu version today. Largest five price warnings are shown, with explicit missing comparison coverage. More than 2000 issued lines prompts a narrower period/outlet. The menu dashboard asks for a narrower search above 1000 eligible records.

Apply `supabase/migrations/202611200045_executive_menu_and_po_exposure.sql` before deploying this app. No PO approval stage is introduced. Existing role and tenant boundaries are preserved. Updated Word/workbook/acceptance pack retains the original 2026-11-19 core dataset and adds two optional PO warning cases after the core checks.
