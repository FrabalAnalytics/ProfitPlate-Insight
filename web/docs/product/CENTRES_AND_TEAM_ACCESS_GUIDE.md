# Centres, team access and waste recording

Current test deployment: migrations through **041** are already applied; do not rerun them. Existing stock history and permissions remain intact. Existing explicit waste access is retained.

## Set up a centre in one place

1. Open **Data & Setup > Centres & team access**. If Main Store, Kitchen or Bar already exists, choose **Edit centre** on its row. This keeps its stock and history.
2. Enter the centre name, storage type and outlet. A centre code is generated from the name; change it under **More centre details** only if needed.
3. Select the **Responsible member**. For an operational colleague, review **Request materials**, **Record waste** and **Accept transfers**, then select **Save centre & access**. The centre and its owner tasks save together. Other centre assignments are preserved.

Existing role-wide access stays intact; assigning its owner connects the Inventory Manager to that centre's waste log. A person without an operational role can still be named as responsible, but needs an appropriate role before recording stock.

Open **Additional staff & detailed permissions** on the same page to assign other colleagues. Allow their centre, then choose their tasks. **Record waste** is independent of **Request materials**. Dispatch, transfer approval and general stock posting remain under **More stock controls** and retain their role restrictions. An Inventory Manager's waste access remains limited to centres they own.

The processing unit and material baselines remain available. Choose the processing unit under **Choose where material processing happens**. Movement routes remain under **Inventory & movements > Locations & routes**.

## Record waste

1. Open **Waste Log > Record waste**. If you have one permitted centre, it is already selected. Your saved display name fills **Submitter name**; check or edit it.
2. Find and choose the material, enter its quantity using the unit shown, then choose a waste reason. Add another material if needed. Notes are optional.
3. Select **Record waste**. No approval or separate posting is required. Stock changes once at the selected centre, and the submitter name is retained alongside the signed-in account.

Record processing trim inside its processing batch rather than entering it again as standalone waste. Existing stock availability, date, tenant and centre checks still apply.
