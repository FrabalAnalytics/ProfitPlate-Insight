# Daily waste accountability and analysis (049)

Apply `supabase/migrations/202611200049_daily_waste_accountability.sql` before deploying the matching application. The migration adds reporting schedules, named daily declarations, immutable loss-estimate snapshots and scoped read APIs. No existing stock balances are recalculated. Existing posted losses receive explicitly labelled estimates reconstructed at rollout; existing daily reporting obligations start at the restaurant's local rollout date rather than inventing historical arrears.

Every active stock centre has a reporting schedule. New centres start on their creation day, every day of the week, with a 23:59 deadline in the restaurant's reporting timezone. Restaurant administrators and organisation-wide CEOs can configure operating days and same-day or next-day deadlines under Waste reporting schedules. Schedule changes are prospective from tomorrow. Replacing future plans keeps their prior values in the audit event; past schedules are retained. Ownership and activation changes apply to future reporting days. Initial owner corrections before any centre history or declaration can complete setup on the creation day.

Workspace reporting status distinguishes pending, in progress, overdue, complete with waste, complete with no waste and closed. Recording alone does not confirm the entire day. An authorised waste-recording colleague confirms completion with a submitter name; the actual signed-in account is captured separately. Confirmation does not require approval and does not move stock. Unposted loss documents must be completed or cancelled first. The no-waste declaration is rejected if posted waste or processing trim exists. Submission tokens, revision checks and evidence hashes protect retries and stale browser tabs.

New waste or processing loss reopens a completed or explicitly closed day, retaining its earlier declaration in history. Manual reopening requires a reason. Exceptional closed-day declarations require a restaurant administrator or CEO, a reason and no waste evidence. They are excluded from expected reporting, while declarations made after the deadline remain visibly flagged. Once confirmed, a day must be explicitly reopened before its declaration changes. Retired centres can finish their historical reports under the existing centre/role restrictions without regaining physical stock-posting permission.

Completion is confirmed centre-days divided by expected operating centre-days. On-time compliance is reports confirmed by their deadlines divided by expected reports whose deadlines have passed. Today's not-yet-due reports are not overdue and do not depress the on-time denominator. Late confirmations resolve the outstanding item but remain late. Scheduled closed days and audited exceptional closures are distinct from no-waste confirmations. Missing owners remain visible as setup gaps.

Inventory Managers retain their responsible-centre scope, Kitchen/Bar users their assigned centre scope, and Finance/CEOs their authorised combined view. Finance does not gain declaration, stock-entry or setup rights. Workspace reminders are in-app, refreshed with the workspace; the feature does not send external emails. Daily history and CSV exports apply the same centre and date permissions as the screen.

Analysis contains recorded loss/value trends, reporting-completion trends, reasons, centre patterns, material quantities/known estimated values and processing yield against pinned baselines. Only posted losses appear in loss analysis. Measured processing trim is included exactly once and does not create a second stock deduction. Production output achievement and measured usable-weight yield are separate from waste entries; yield gaps are not automatically converted into waste.

Loss estimates are captured at posting using dated monthly purchase averages, starting prices or recipe-derived estimates as available. Missing prices remain missing, including after later price corrections; existing snapshots are not silently revalued. Known value is a partial subtotal whenever unpriced entries exist, not inventory valuation or actual profit. Quantities retain their own units. Older unclassified reasons are not guessed. A confirmed zero-loss day can plot zero; absent reports and all-unpriced days do not become zero values. Coverage accompanies every time series, and reconstructed historical values are disclosed.

The complete testing pack includes a daily declaration collection sheet and daily accountability acceptance cases. Physical operations and daily reporting use the actual operational test date in the restaurant timezone, while the pack's historical financial sample dates remain explicit.
