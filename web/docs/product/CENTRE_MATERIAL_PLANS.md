# Centre Material Plan (051)

Apply `supabase/migrations/202611200051_centre_material_plans.sql` before deploying the matching app. It adds read-only planning functions. No stock, recipe, financial or historical records are changed by generating a plan.

Material Plan appears in Daily operations and the operational workspace tools. Users select an active permitted centre, plan date, up to 30 distinct saved recipes/baselines and target output quantities. Targets use the recipe's declared output unit, not batch counts. The latest version effective on the plan date is selected per recipe; a changed/stale selection is rejected rather than silently replaced. Future dates within one year are allowed, while available stock is explicitly the current on-hand quantity less reservations, not a forecast.

Stock managers and centre users with request access can plan within their current restaurant/centre permissions. Planning is available at storage/service centres without enabling production. Finance-only, receiving-only, unrelated-centre and anonymous users gain no planning authority. A valid plan with insufficient stock is still generated.

Keep stocked preparations as SKUs uses a permitted baseline's pinned inputs where available. For other recipes, a bound saved child recipe becomes its tracked output material; an unbound child expands through its pinned composition. Expand saved sub-recipe ingredients expands pinned child recipes instead. Direct material components, including explicitly listed processed SKUs, remain material requirements in both modes. The module does not recursively turn every direct processed material into its raw production inputs. This prevents the same preparation and its raw components being required twice for one demand. Selecting a child as another top-level target means additional demand.

Requirements are consolidated by material identity in base units, including packaging. No unlike-unit grand total is shown. Untracked materials show no invented available balance or shortage. Quantities retain database decimals in CSV; consolidated required quantities round upwards at 12 decimal places. Missing yields, invalid/inactive materials, invalid scope/units and cyclic/excessive nesting produce a clear error instead of a partial or zero-filled plan. Recursive expansion is bounded to 20 levels and 5,000 component steps per selected recipe, with a 1,000-material consolidated limit.

The result identifies restaurant, centre, plan date, generation time, recipe versions/targets, required materials, available-now quantities and shortages. Users can inspect per-recipe requirements, download formula-safe CSV or print. Editing inputs hides the prior result until regeneration. Plans are generated snapshots, not persisted planning documents or reservations; users retain them by export/print. No new requisition or production order is created automatically.

Regression coverage checks common-material consolidation, fractional batch scaling, baseline inputs, stocked versus expanded children, unit conversion, shortage-tolerant generation, unchanged ledger/documents, invalid targets and scope/role boundaries. Browser coverage generates a two-recipe plan with zero stock, downloads its CSV, checks stale-result handling and verifies narrow-screen layout. The updated full guide and training pack include PLAN-01 through PLAN-03.
