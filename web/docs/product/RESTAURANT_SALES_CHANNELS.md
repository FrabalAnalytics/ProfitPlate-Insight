# Restaurant-owned sales channels - release 046

Current onboarding requires the owner to select or create at least one channel at the Brand & first location step. Choices are saved in the draft, reviewed before completion, and applied atomically when the workspace is created. Names and codes are not restricted to a global three-value list. Codes allow letters, numbers, underscores and hyphens (40 characters); ALL is reserved for the comparison filter. Existing tenants keep their current three channels, prices and sales. Compatibility defaults for legacy registration callers are replaced within the current onboarding transaction by the owner's exact selections; completion retries cannot reset later configuration changes.

Data & Setup > Sales channels allows administrator-only name edits, additions, retirement and reactivation with version checks. At least one active channel is required. Existing codes are read-only in the UI and historical records are never rewritten or deleted. Price and sales rows reference the channel within the same restaurant. Direct configuration writes and cross-tenant access are denied. Updates are audited.

Menu profitability defaults to All channels, showing one separate row per active menu/channel combination. Totals and target summaries count comparisons, not unique dishes; no prices, costs or percentages are averaged across channels. A row's breakdown opens its concrete channel and Back preserves the all-channel selection. Retired channels can be selected individually for historical inspection. New prices use active channels only. Missing prices remain incomplete. At most 5000 combinations are calculated per search; larger scopes require a narrower search or a single channel.

Menu pricing, sales import mappings and overview labels use the restaurant configuration. Sales staging and posting validate channel ownership/activity. Retirement invalidates an unposted new-sales preview; exact reimports of already recorded retired-channel receipts remain duplicates rather than adding sales. Existing immutable source files and recorded numbers remain unchanged.

Apply `supabase/migrations/202611200046_restaurant_sales_channels.sql` before the matching app. The updated Word guide and test pack include a manual sales-channel collection sheet and two acceptance cases. This does not introduce universal master-data uploads or default-price inheritance.
